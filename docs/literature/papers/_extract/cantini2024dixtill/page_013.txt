•	 Feature agreement. It is computed as the fraction of common features between the 
sets of top-k attributions of ET(x) and ES(x) of a given student model S. Formally: 
1
k |{f ∈F | f ∈topf (ET(x), k) ∧f ∈topf (ES(x), k)}|.
•	 Sign agreement. It measures to what extent ET(x) and ES(x) of a given stu-
dent model S agree by also considering the feature attribution signs for 
the top-k features. Therefore, this is a stricter measure, computed as: 
1
k |{f ∈F | f ∈topf (ET(x), k) ∧f ∈topf (ES(x), k) ∧sgn(ET(x), f )=sgn(ES(x), f )}|.
