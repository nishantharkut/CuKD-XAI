Table 7 shows the parameters and the values used in
this paper for WEKA toolbox Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)
ANN classifier configuration. MLP is the most popular ANN
variation that allows configuration of multilayer ANN which
is able to model complex relations between the input and
output parameters.
Several ANN architectures were attempted in this paper,
an ANN with one hidden layer and 11 neurons is used.
Moreover, an ANN with two hidden layers with 11 neurons in
the first layer and 5 neurons in the second hidden layer was
used. Finally, ANN with three hidden layers with 11 neurons
in the first layer, 5 neurons in the second hidden layer, and
two neurons in the third hidden layer was also attempted.
