3) PERFORMANCE COMPARISON WITH OTHER ML AND DL
MODELS
The effectiveness of the SA-DCNN model was affirmed
through a comprehensive validation process, which involved
comparing its outcomes with those of various cutting-edge
methods. For comparison, traditional ML and sophisticated
DL models were employed, encompassing the multi-layer
perceptron (MLP), gaussian naive Bayes (GNB), linear
regression (LR), deep-autoencoder (DAE), LSTM, GRU, and
CNN. It’s noteworthy that these models were executed within
the same environment, incorporating identical preprocessing
steps as the proposed model. This approach ensured an
equitable and meaningful assessment of their respective
performances. All the implemented DL models utilized the
sparse categorical cross-entropy loss function, employed the
Adam optimizer, and were trained with a batch size of 32.
