4.5. TCN Training Experiment Results
Table 3 shows that the TCN training model achieved the best accuracy, recall, and F1
score when the expansion factor d was 8 and the kernel size w was 1. As the expansion
factor d increased, the number of layers and parameters in the model increased, leading to
an increase in training parameters. Considering all factors, this paper selected a parameter
configuration of expansion factor d = 8 and kernel size w = 1.
