The computational complexity of the proposed federated
learning approach for IoT botnet attack detection is opti-
mized to balance efﬁciency, scalability, and the constraints
of IoT environments. Local model training, employing Ran-
dom Forest classiﬁers, scales with O(t · n · log(n)) per tree,
where t is the number of trees and n is the number of samples
per client. This ensures efﬁcient training on non-IID client
datasets while distributing computational demands across
devices. The SHAP-based feature importance computation,
