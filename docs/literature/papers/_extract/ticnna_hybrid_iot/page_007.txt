B. TIGAN TRAINING AND BALANCED DATA
The TIGAN model was trained for 5000–10000 epochs
based on different classes taking 360-800 seconds of training
time for each minority sub-class. Figure 9 depicts the loss
graph for discriminator and generator on subclasses.
The
TIGAN model was trained on 33 distinct subclasses using
hybrid sampling techniques to enhance image generation
quality, producing 170 threshold images for each class. A pie
chart, Fig. 10. visualises the distribution of these images,
highlighting class representation and revealing insights into
performance and areas needing refinement. The results
confirm the successful application of the TIGAN model in
generating relevant images. Overall, these findings under-
score TIGAN’s potential to create high-quality, subclass-
specific images and contribute significantly to research in
synthetic image generation.
