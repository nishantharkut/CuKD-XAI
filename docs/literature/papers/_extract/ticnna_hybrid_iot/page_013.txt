4) TICNN LAYER MODEL ARCHITECTURE ANALYSIS
The ablation study was conducted to assess the impact
of various modifications in the model architecture on the
performance of the classification tasks for the CICIoT2023
and CICIDS2017 datasets. The baseline model achieved the
highest accuracy of 99.56% for CICIoT2023 and 99.98%
for CICIDS2017, demonstrating the effectiveness of the fully
implemented architecture, including all convolutional blocks,
dropout layers, and dense units.
In the ‘‘no dropout’’ scenario, removing the dropout
layers caused the model’s accuracy to significantly drop to
68% for CICIoT2023 and 54% for CICIDS2017, indicating
the critical role of dropout in preventing overfitting and
maintaining generalization. For other ablation configurations,
such as reducing convolutional layers or dense units,
the accuracy was slightly lower than the baseline but did
not drop as drastically as in the ‘‘no dropout’’ case. This
indicates that while reducing model complexity slightly
impacts performance, the overall architecture remains robust.
The results highlight the importance of each architectural
component in achieving high classification accuracy and the
trade-offs between model complexity and performance.
