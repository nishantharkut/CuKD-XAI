The results in TABLE 3 compare the performance of
the models VGG16, VGG19, Xception, and TICNN on
imbalanced and balanced dataset for CICIoT2023. For the
imbalanced dataset, all models exhibited high accuracy, but
Xception had the lowest macro average precision at 0.722.
VGG19 and VGG16 followed with scores of 0.784 and 0.765,
respectively. In contrast, the TICNN model significantly
outperformed the others across all weighted average metrics,
achieving a precision, recall, and F1-score of 0.998. Its macro
average precision was 0.855, the highest among the models,
indicating its effectiveness in classifying minority instances
despite the imbalance.
When using a balanced dataset, all models showed
improved performance. The VGG16, VGG19, and Xception
models demonstrated better uniformity in performance,
achieving macro average scores of 0.970, 0.967, and 0.959,
respectively. The TICNN model maintained its lead with a
weighted average precision of 0.997, recall of 0.995, and
F1-score of 0.996. Its macro average precision and recall also
improved to 0.994 and 0.995, respectively. Packet detection
times for CICIoT2023 ranged from 1.5 ms to 2.92 ms.
