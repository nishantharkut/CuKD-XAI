As an example, ﬁrst we examine the SHAP force plots
for SYN Flood and Slowloris attack samples, depicted in
Figs.6 to 7. In the SYN Flood plot, the base value starts at
3.36, reﬂecting the model’s initial prediction before incor-
porating feature contributions. The ﬁnal output value of
15.64 is achieved primarily due to the substantial contri-
butions of features such as ‘syn_flag_number’ and
‘Number’, which strongly push the prediction toward
the malicious class. Similarly, in the Slowloris plot, the
base value starts at −0.06675, indicating a baseline heav-
ily leaning towards a benign outcome. The ﬁnal output
value increases sharply to 9.94, primarily driven by the
‘Number’ and ‘fin_count’ features, which have sig-
niﬁcant positive contributions towards the malicious class.
This pattern underscores the model’s capability to identify
slow DDoS attacks like Slowloris that rely on speciﬁc trafﬁc
patterns. Although less prominent in the plot, features such
as ‘syn_count’ and ‘IAT’ also exhibit small positive
SHAP values, contributing to the overall prediction.
