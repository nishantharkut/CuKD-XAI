Multi-class Classification Results.
• Accuracy: The LENSXAI Student achieved the highest accuracy of
99.34%, surpassing all competing models, including RNN-IDS (81.29%)

Naive Bayes (76.1% precision) and Random Forest (75.9% recall) [51].

ues of 98.64% and 98.67%, respectively.
• F1-Measure: Both Teacher (98.63%) and Student (98.44%) models
maintained balanced performance, outperforming deep learning meth-
ods like MCNN-DFS (80.00%) [51] and STL-IDS (79.08%) [44].
