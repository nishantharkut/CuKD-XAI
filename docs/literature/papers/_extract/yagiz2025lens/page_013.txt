3.5.3. Order Sensitivity and Approximation
The contributions v(j, x∗) depend on the order of the variables j, as
interactions between variables may influence the result. To address this, we
use a two-step heuristic [36, 37]:
1. Variables are ordered based on their marginal importance |∆j|∅(x∗)|,
calculated without prior conditioning.
2. Contributions v(j, x∗) are then computed sequentially using the chosen
order, capturing both individual and interaction effects.
By implementing the variable attribution method, as outlined in Algo-
rithm 1, the framework enables robust, feature-level explanations, empow-
ering analysts to make data-driven decisions and refine IDS configurations
effectively.
