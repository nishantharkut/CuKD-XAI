• Recall: Both LENSXAI Teacher and Student achieved outstanding
recall scores of 98.42% and 98.12%, respectively, ensuring high detec-
tion rates for malicious activities. These results outperform traditional
methods such as NB (76.00%) and LR (86.00%) [40].
• Precision: The precision scores of 98.43% for the Teacher model and
98.14% for the Student model highlight their capability to minimize
false positives, ensuring reliability in real-world applications.
• F1 Score and Balanced Performance: The harmonic mean of pre-
cision and recall for LENSXAI Teacher and Student were 98.42% and
98.12%, respectively, showcasing balanced and consistent performance
in intrusion detection tasks.
