C.5
Does showing the student ground truth labels improve ﬁdelity?
In Figure 14 we investigate the effect of the relative weight of the distillation loss terms LNLL and
LKD when distilling teacher-ensembles with ResNet56 components into a ResNet56 student on
CIFAR-100 with τ = 1. We observe that in the self-distillation regime taking α > 0 improves test
accuracy, but not test agreement. When k > 0, there is a slight beneﬁt when α = 0.1, but for most
values tried the effect was deleterious to both accuracy and ﬁdelity.
