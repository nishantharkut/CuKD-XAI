C. PERFORMANCE METRICS
Performance indicators are essential for evaluating a
machine-learning model’s efficacy. They are especially
important for assessing the results of categorization models.
These metrics provide insights into how well the model
produces predictions and help the comparison of alternative
models or changes to the existing one. The performance
matrices used to analyze the proposed work are discussed
below:
1) ACCURACY
The most widely used performance indicator for determining
the percentage of accurate predictions produced by the ML
model is accuracy. It can be calculated as:

4) F1 SCORE
The F1 score is a balanced statistic that considers both
precision and recall, calculated as the harmonic mean of
these two metrics. With a single statistic that represents the
model’s performance in terms of both false positives and false
negatives, it is particularly helpful for balancing the trade-off
between precision and recall. F1 score is calculated as:

Certain performance measures can be quite helpful in
certain situations. For example, whereas accuracy might be
adequate in many circumstances, precision becomes essential
when the cost of false positives is significant. However, recall
is crucial when it comes to missing a real positive because it
may be very expensive. When a balance between recall and
precision is required, the F1 score is useful as it provides a
thorough evaluation of the model’s performance.
