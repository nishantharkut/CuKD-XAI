V. CONCLUSION
This research presents a highly effective and scalable IDS for
WSN, combining cutting-edge ML techniques and optimiza-
tion strategies. The proposed system, leveraging PSO and an
ensemble of Random Forest, Decision Tree, and K-Nearest
Neighbors models, achieved an exceptional accuracy of
99.73%, with precision, recall, and F1-score all reaching
99.72%. The use of advanced data balancing methods like
SMOTE-Tomek significantly improved the detection of mi-
nority attack instances, overcoming the challenges posed by
dataset imbalance. Additionally, explainable AI techniques
such as SHAP and LIME provided crucial insights into the
model’s decision-making process, enhancing transparency
and interpretability, key for practical applications. Moreover,
the integration of the Streamlit web application allowed
for an intuitive and interactive interface, enabling real-time
validation of the model’s predictions across different attack
types and normal conditions, further demonstrating the
model’s robustness and user accessibility.Future work should
focus on optimizing the system’s computational efficiency
for deployment in resource-constrained environments and
extending its applicability to broader, more diverse datasets
to ensure greater generalizability across different WSN
scenarios.
