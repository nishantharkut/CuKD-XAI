The ROC curves are a complete measure of the models’ dif-
ferentiating capabilities by showing the relationship between
the True Positive Rate (TPR) and the False Positive Rate
(FPR) for different decision thresholds. As the AUC values
approach one, better classiﬁcation performances are indi-
cated along with a lower difﬁculty of differentiating between
attack classes and benign trafﬁc. The DCQI conﬁguration is
the one that achieves the highest AUC in all categories, thus
demonstrating its capability of elevating the sensitivity lev-
els without reducing the speciﬁcity, an immediate effect of
the QIO-driven decision boundary optimization. Apart from
that, DCOA also shows a signiﬁcant increment compared
to DNCA; however, the AUC values are somewhat lower
than those of DCQI, which agrees with the fact that its opti-
mization strategy is more directed towards exploration. The
baseline DNCA, which still has a high AUC in “Normal”
and less complex attack types, decreases its performance in
highly variable intrusion patterns such as “Flooding.” In gen-
eral, the diagram corroborates the numerical indicators by
illustrating that metaheuristic integration, particularly QIO,
achieves classiﬁer robustness across various types of intru-
sion scenarios in wireless sensor networks.
