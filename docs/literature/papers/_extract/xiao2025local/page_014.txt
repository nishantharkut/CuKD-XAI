federated detection systems, which can be more suitable for
privacy-sensitive or geographically dispersed deployments,
were not part of the study’s focus.
5.2 Future directions
Due to these constraints, the researchers will concentrate on
broadening the design to include online and adaptive learning
paradigms. This will allow the system to change its decision
boundaries on the ﬂy as new and evolving attack vectors are
encountered. Moreover, the adoption of federated learning
might alleviate issues related to both scalability and privacy,
while the use of lightweight deep learning (e.g., quantized or
pruned networks) could make it easier for ultra-low-power
sensornodestobedeployed.Futureworkwillalsoinvestigate
enhanced anomaly detection strategies and noise-resilient
learning to improve the detection of completely novel attacks
and mitigate the impact of data corruption. Future works
will focus on multimodal intrusion detecting, integrating
cross-layer and contextual information that will improve the
system’s resistance to sophisticated and untraceable cyber-
attacks. To recap, this work offers a deep-ensemble-based
intrusion detection system optimized with metaheuristics
which is both computationally efﬁcient and interpretable.
Apart from being a potential avenue for the development
of next-generation security systems for wireless sensor net-
works that are not only robust, resource-aware, and adaptable
to the ever-increasing complexity and adversarial landscape
of wireless sensing environments, this framework also signi-
ﬁes such a fund.
