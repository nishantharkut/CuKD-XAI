robust performance metrics, with KFCV and SS achieving comparable accuracy, precision, and F1 scores 
across models. However, KFCV (k=5) demonstrates slightly better consistency in minimizing mean absolute 
error (MAE) and root mean square error (RMSE), particularly for the RFC model, which achieved the highest 
performance metrics (99.94% accuracy, precision, and F1 score). The stratification inherent in both methods 
ensures class balance, but KFCV’s iterative training on multiple folds enhances generalizability and reduces 
variance, making it a more robust choice for scenarios involving diverse or imbalanced datasets.
