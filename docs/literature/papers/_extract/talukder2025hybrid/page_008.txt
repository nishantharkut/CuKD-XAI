more suitable choice for datasets with complex distributions and varying levels of class imbalance.
Dimension reduction using PCA
Principal Component Analysis (PCA) is a statistical technique used for reducing the dimensionality of a dataset 
by transforming it into a new coordinate system, where the axes (principal components) represent directions of 
maximum variance in the data. PCA works by finding the eigenvectors and eigenvalues of the covariance matrix 
of the data. The eigenvectors define the principal components, and the corresponding eigenvalues measure the 
variance along these components. PCA transforms the data into a lower-dimensional space by selecting a subset 
of components that capture the most variance.
