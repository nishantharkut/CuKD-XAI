Practical implementation of IDS in real-world scenarios
Our research addresses the dynamic nature of WSN environments and evolving attack patterns by presenting a 
robust and adaptable intrusion detection framework. We employ advanced data processing techniques, including 
StandardScaler for feature standardization, KMeans SMOTE for handling class imbalance, and PCA with the 
Kaiser Criterion for dimensionality reduction, to efficiently process diverse attack scenarios. By validating our 
approach on benchmark datasets such as WSN-DS and TON-IoT, we demonstrate its effectiveness in real-
world scenarios. Furthermore, the lightweight design of our machine learning models ensures compatibility 
with resource-constrained WSN environments, offering a scalable and efficient solution for real-time intrusion 
detection.
