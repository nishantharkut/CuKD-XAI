StandardScaler
The StandardScaler is a preprocessing technique used to normalize features by removing the mean and scaling 
to unit variance. It ensures that the features have a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1, making the data 
well-suited for ML algorithms sensitive to feature magnitudes. The standardization of a feature x is computed as:

where x: Original feature value; µ: Mean of the feature; σ: Standard deviation of the feature.
StandardScaler improves the model by ensuring that features are on a comparable scale, thereby allowing ML 
algorithms to converge faster and avoid biases due to varying feature magnitudes. Additionally, it enhances 
numerical stability, preventing issues arising from extreme values. The StandardScaler is particularly beneficial 
for distance-based models, such as clustering algorithms, where feature scaling directly impacts the calculation 
of distances.
Label encoding
Label Encoding is a preprocessing technique used to convert categorical labels into numerical values. Each 
unique category is assigned a specific integer value, enabling ML algorithms to process non-numerical labels 
effectively.
Let y represent the categorical labels. The encoding maps each unique category c to a corresponding integer
