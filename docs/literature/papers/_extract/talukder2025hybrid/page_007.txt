STL addresses both the imbalance and noise in datasets by combining oversampling and undersampling, 
reducing the risk of model overfitting and misclassification. STL enhances IDS models’ recall and precision by 
generating realistic synthetic samples while eliminating noisy instances, improving overall detection rates.
GAN—generative adversarial network
Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are DL-based methods that generate synthetic samples for the 
minority class using a generator and discriminator model33. The generator creates synthetic samples, while the 
discriminator evaluates their authenticity against real samples. The generator learns to produce realistic samples 
that the discriminator cannot distinguish from real data.

KMS—KMeansSMOTE
KMeansSMOTE is a clustering-based oversampling technique that applies K-Means clustering to partition data 
before generating synthetic samples using SMOTE. The dataset is first clustered using K-Means, and SMOTE 
is applied within each cluster to generate synthetic samples for the minority class. This localized oversampling 
preserves the original data distribution and reduces noise.
