Adversarial Networks (GAN), and K-Means Sampling (KMS) and Principal Component Analysis (PCA) for 
efficiently reducing the high-dimensional feature space while retaining the most significant information. Metrics

achieving superior accuracy in detecting attacks effectively. This highlights the importance of selecting the 
appropriate data balancing technique while retaining the most significant information and model for improving 
the accuracy and reliability of IDS systems.
