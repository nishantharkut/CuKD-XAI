4.4. IoTID20

model with its complexity, achieves relatively lower accuracy (92.95%) and F1 score (93.13%)
compared to results on other datasets. This suggests that the IoTID20 dataset, with its
device-specific patterns and potential noise or imbalance, poses greater difficulty for even
